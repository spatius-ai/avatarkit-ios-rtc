# NNNN. Title in the imperative

## Context

The problem and its constraints. 2-5 sentences. Self-contained — a reader must
not need another document to follow this.

## Decision

What was chosen. 1-3 sentences.

## Alternatives considered

Each option that was genuinely investigated, and why it was rejected.

**This section is mandatory.** It is the only part of the record a diff can never
reconstruct: the diff shows what was chosen, never what was ruled out and why.

## Consequences

recur (an extra release step, a manual check) — those are what get forgotten.
